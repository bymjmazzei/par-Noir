pN stands for par Noir. It is your digital identity: the root layer. Dashboard, browser, messaging, and third-party tools are built on top of that identity. They are not the identity itself.

par Noir is an ecosystem designed to protect your data using real-world identity principles. In the real world, you have an identity that you own. No company issues it to you as a rented account, and no company gets to take it away. Online, that root is your pN: you create it, you own it, and we do not control it.

Everything else sits on top of that identity. Your files stay in your cloud, stored encrypted and unlocked with your pN. The dashboard, social layer, and third-party platforms use that base with your permission. They can ask for access. They do not become the owner of who you are or what you create.
