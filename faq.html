FAQs

pN stands for par Noir. It’s your digital identity: something you create and hold, not an account a company issues to you.

A set of protocols around identity you own. You create a pN. Your content stays in your cloud. Apps sit on top of that, aggregate what you allow, and don’t host or own your data.

par Noir isn’t a platform. It’s a collection of protocols: identity you hold, content in your cloud, and apps that get permission instead of ownership. Traditional platforms issue your account, host your files, and keep you inside their walls. Here the identity and storage stay with you, and products build on top of that.

If an app shuts down or bans you, your identity and files still sit with you. Apps can use what you share. They don’t keep ownership of it.

Your pN and your stuff can move across tools built on it. Switching apps doesn’t mean starting over as someone else’s user.
